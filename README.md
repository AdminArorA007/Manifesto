# The Ethical Web Manifesto
### By IT-INDIA.org

We believe the internet should be open, accessible, private and free from unnecessary restrictions.

We build web applications for people, not for advertisers, data collectors or monetisation schemes.

Every website and web application developed under the IT-INDIA.org banner should follow these principles.

## Our Core Principles

1. **100% Free and Open Source Software (FOSS).** Our software must be genuinely free and open source, giving users the freedom to use, inspect, modify and redistribute it in accordance with its licence.

2. **No Signups.** No registrations, mandatory user accounts or unnecessary authentication.

3. **No Cookies or Tracking.** No tracking cookies, behavioural profiling or invasive user monitoring.

4. **No Advertisements.** Our applications will never display advertisements.

5. **No Watermarks.** No artificial branding, watermarks or restrictions imposed on user-generated output.

6. **No Donations.** No donation buttons, donation requests or solicitation of financial contributions.

7. **No Paywalls.** No premium features, paid upgrades or subscription-based functionality.

8. **Always Available on GitHub.** Source code must remain publicly accessible on GitHub.

9. **100% Ethical Development and Operation.** We will develop and operate our applications responsibly, honestly and transparently.

10. **No Collection of Personal Information.** We will not collect personal information unless it is strictly essential for the application's functionality.

11. **No Third-Party Trackers or Analytics.** No Google Analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting services or similar tracking technologies.

12. **No Artificial Limitations.** No arbitrary usage quotas, deliberately restricted features or artificial throttling.

13. **No Dark Patterns.** No deceptive interfaces, manipulative buttons, misleading messages or forced interactions.

14. **Security First.** We prioritise secure coding practices, responsible dependency management and protection against common web vulnerabilities.

15. **Accessibility Matters.** We strive to make our applications accessible to everyone, following established accessibility standards wherever reasonably possible.

16. **Mobile-Friendly and Responsive.** Our applications should work properly across desktops, tablets and mobile devices.

17. **No Vendor Lock-in.** Users should remain free to use, modify, migrate and self-host our software without unnecessary proprietary restrictions.

18. **No Unnecessary External Dependencies.** We avoid unnecessary third-party scripts, services, libraries and external resources that compromise privacy or reliability.

19. **Transparent Development.** We publish our source code, licensing information, dependencies and known limitations.

20. **No Forced Upgrades.** We will never deliberately disable a working version simply to force users to upgrade.

21. **Respect User Data.** We will not sell, share or monetise user data. Users should retain control over their information.

22. **No Unnecessary Telemetry.** Our applications will not secretly transmit usage statistics, behavioural information or other unnecessary data.

23. **Self-Hosting Friendly.** Wherever technically feasible, our applications should be straightforward to install and operate on your own server.

24. **Built for Users, Not Monetisation.** Our primary objective is to solve problems and provide useful tools. We will never compromise our principles for commercial gain.

## Our Promise

**The internet belongs to everyone.**

We believe useful software should not come with a price tag, a tracking script, an advertisement, a registration form or an obligation.

We build because we can. We share because we believe in an open internet.

**No strings attached. No hidden agenda. Just useful software, freely available to everyone.**
